# Stanford Chinese Editions · Stanford 课程资料中文版

## Source | 来源

Use the supplied edition to establish the file list, reading order and numbering. Verify the edition and text of any editable source. Work on a separate copy. Follow the requested destination and course naming rules, using the defaults below for anything unspecified.

以指定原件核定文件清单、阅读顺序和编号。使用可编辑源稿前核对版本与正文。在副本上制作译本。交付位置和名称遵循本次任务与课程约定，未指定的部分采用下文默认规则。

## Translation | 翻译

1. Translate headings, prose, worked steps, captions, table cells, labels and footnotes in place. Preserve definitions, qualifications and cross-references. Distinguish requirements, recommendations and examples as the source does. Use one Chinese term per concept and retain the English term where it resolves ambiguity.
   在对应位置翻译标题、正文、解题步骤、图注、表格、标注和脚注。保留定义、限定条件和交叉引用，准确区分原文的要求、建议与示例。同一概念统一译名，有歧义时附英文原词。
2. Preserve formulas, subscripts, superscripts, units, figures, datasets, code identifiers, commands, URLs and bibliographic details. In a package, mirror the tree, translate descriptive names and update their references. Preserve filenames required by builds, imports or grading.
   保留公式、上下标、单位、图形、数据集、代码标识符、命令、网址和引文信息。整包译本逐项对应原目录，翻译描述性名称并同步修复引用。保留构建、导入或评分所依赖的文件名。
3. Follow the source’s visual hierarchy. Match heading levels, emphasis, figure and table placement, and the meaning of colors and symbols. Adjust line and page breaks in Chinese to keep captions with their figures and tables. Deliver the translation beside its source by default, appending `（中文版）` before the extension or after the package name.
   沿用原稿的视觉层级，包括标题层次、重点标记、图表位置以及颜色和符号的含义。按中文阅读需要调整换行和分页，使图表与题注相邻。默认与原件并列，单文件在扩展名前追加 `（中文版）`，整包在目录名后追加。

## Verification | 验收

Compare sections, equations, exercises, captions and assets against the source. For PDFs, render the translation and inspect dense pages for clipped text, missing glyphs and displaced labels. Open internal links and check references to renamed files. Identify any unreadable source passage by page or section.

对照原件逐项核对章节、公式、习题、图注和资源。交付 PDF 时渲染译本，检查密排页的截字、缺字及标注错位。打开内部链接，检查改名文件的引用。遇到无法辨读的原文，注明页码或小节。
