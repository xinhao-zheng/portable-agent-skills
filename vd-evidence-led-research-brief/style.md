# Language: Skill Doc + Public Brief · 语言风格：Skill 文档＋公开短稿

Use two voices, separated by artifact type. Both are self-contained. The skill
inherits one authorship system; it does not force one surface register onto every
artifact.
按工件类型使用两种声线，且二者均自包含。Skill 继承同一作者系统；不把一种表层语体强压
到所有产物上。

---

## Voice A — Skill documentation | Voice A——Skill 文档

Write `SKILL.md`, `logic.md`, and this file in the catalog's austere analytical
voice: narrow the task, advance by dependency or cause, use load-bearing words,
state the failure if a gate is skipped, and close only after the boundary is clear.
`SKILL.md`、`logic.md` 与本文件使用目录统一的冷峻思辨文档体：收窄任务，以依赖或因果
推进，每词承重，点明跳过闸门会怎样，边界清楚后才允许收束。

1. **Zero filler.** Cut “comprehensive / in-depth / worth noting”. Name the
   question, gate, input, failure, or output.
   **零套话。** 删“全面／深入／值得注意”。直接写问题、闸门、输入、失败或输出。
2. **Concrete verbs.** Use bind, align, trace, test, derive, preserve, compress,
   synchronize — not “conduct / carry out / make clearer”.
   **具象动词。** 用收窄、对齐、追溯、检验、推出、保留、压缩、同步——不用“进行／开展／
   使其更清晰”。
3. **Qualification earns the em dash.** Use it to narrow or reverse a claim, not
   as decoration.
   **破折号承载限定。** 只用于收窄或反转主张，不作装饰。
4. **Honesty over force.** “The evidence does not identify this” is a result, not
   an apology.
   **诚实压过强硬。** “现有证据无法识别”是结果，不是道歉。

### One-pass clean (skill docs) | 一键清洗（skill 文档）

1. Delete filler and mood-adjectives. | 删套话与情绪形容词。
2. Replace “clear / rigorous” with the gate and the failure it prevents. |
   把“清晰／严谨”换成具体闸门及其防止的失败。
3. Check that every fingerprint item maps to the logic step with the same number. |
   检查每个 fingerprint 项是否与同编号 logic 步骤对应。
4. Remove task-specific companies, metrics, paths, and conclusions from general
   rules. | 从通用规则中删去任务专属公司、指标、路径与结论。

## Voice B — Vertex Dimension public brief | Voice B——Vertex Dimension 公开短稿

Write the brief **deep backstage, plain upfront, concise throughout**. A broad
reader should understand the answer and its reason without decoding the method; a
specialist should still locate the source, reproduce the load-bearing calculation,
and see where inference begins. Never name a skill in the deliverable.
短稿须**后台研究深、前台表达白、全文保持精炼**。普通读者无需解码方法也能理解答案与理由；
专业读者仍能定位来源、复算承重计算，并看出推断从哪里开始。交付物中绝不点名任何 skill。

The target lies between four failures:
目标位于四种失败之间：

- ✗ **academic fog · 学术迷雾** — method and jargon arrive before meaning · 方法与术语挡在意思之前；
- ✗ **audit dump · 审计堆砌** — every number survives into the public text · 所有数字原样进入正文；
- ✗ **chatty simplification · 闲聊式简化** — readable, but the causal bridge disappears · 好读，但因果桥消失；
- ✗ **AI insight tone · AI 洞见腔** — repeated “not A but B” reversals without new evidence · 反复“不是 A 而是 B”，却无新增证据；
- ✓ **right · 对** — plain answer → exact evidence → visible bridge → rival and limit → conditional close ·
  白话答案 → 精确证据 → 可见桥梁 → 竞争解释与边界 → 条件收束。

### Answer before method | 答案先于方法

1. **Use the first screen for the answer, reason, and limit.** The opening may
   state a question, but it must answer it immediately. Do not lead with “this
   article uses four steps”, a source inventory, or a literature review.
   **第一屏交付答案、理由与边界。** 开篇可以提出问题，但须立即回答。不得以“本文分四步”、
   来源清单或文献回顾领起。
   - ✓ *“The pilot improved one stage sharply; the system improved only slightly,
     because the queue moved downstream.”* · “试点显著改善了一个环节；系统只小幅改善，
     因为队列向下游转移了。”
   - ✗ *“This article constructs a multidimensional framework and proceeds in four
     stages.”* · “本文构建多维框架，并分四步展开。”

2. **Make one paragraph do one inferential job.** Begin with the human meaning,
   follow with the minimum evidence, and end with the consequence or next question.
   **一段只做一项推断工作。** 先讲人话意思，再放最少必要证据，最后落到后果或下一问。

### Let evidence status sound different | 让证据状态听得出差别

3. **Match verbs to status.** “The filing states” marks observation; “subtracting
   A from B gives” marks calculation; “under these assumptions” marks estimate;
   “taken together, these suggest” marks inference; “if X” marks scenario; “the
   public record does not identify” marks unknown.
   **让动词匹配状态。** “公告显示”标观察；“B 减 A 得”标计算；“在这些假设下”标估计；
   “两项合看可推”标推断；“若 X”标情景；“公开资料无法识别”标未知。

4. **Calibrate strong words.** “Only / proves / real / rational / pure sentiment”
   require eliminated rivals. Otherwise use “better explains / is consistent
   with / supports / remains tentative”.
   **校准强词。** “唯一／证明／真实／理性／纯情绪”须以排除竞争解释为前提；否则写
   “解释力更强／与……一致／支持／仍属暂定”。

### Keep plain language load-bearing | 白话，但每词承重

5. **Explain a term once, in a clause a colleague would use.** Give the plain
   meaning first, then the technical term in parentheses if it helps. Do not dumb
   down; remove the decoding burden.
   **术语只拆一次，用同事会说的话。** 先讲白话含义，必要时再把术语放进括号。不是降智，
   是移走解码负担。
   - ✓ *“Free cash flow means the cash left after operating inflow pays for
     capital spending.”* · “自由现金流，说白了是经营流入付完资本开支后还剩多少现金。”

6. **Use named subjects and concrete verbs.** Say who pays, delays, reprices,
   approves, supplies, absorbs, or passes on the risk. Avoid abstract noun chains.
   **使用具名主语与具象动词。** 写清谁付款、拖延、重定价、审批、供给、吸收或转嫁风险；
   避免抽象名词串。

7. **Keep dialogue measured.** Ask the reader's real question at a turn, then
   answer it. Do not simulate intimacy with “obviously / scared yet / you know”,
   and do not repeat rhetorical questions for momentum.
   **对话感须克制。** 转折处替读者问出真实问题，再回答；不用“显然／怕了吗／你懂的”
   模拟亲近，也不用连续反问制造气势。

### Show only the calculation the conclusion needs | 只展示结论需要的计算

8. **Explain the mechanism before the formula.** When a calculation is
   load-bearing, use a 100-unit or similarly intuitive example first; then give
   the formula, inputs, result, meaning, and largest sensitivity. Put full
   derivations backstage when they do not change reader trust.
   **公式前先讲机制。** 计算承重时，先用“100 单位”等直观例子，再给公式、输入、结果、
   含义与最大敏感项。完整推导不改变读者信任时，移到后台。

9. **Keep sources close without building a citation wall.** Name source, date,
   and section or equivalent location beside the fact. If the user requests no
   links, keep the public prose link-free and place full addresses in the evidence
   note; do not remove provenance.
   **来源贴近主张，但不筑引用墙。** 在事实旁点名来源、日期与章节／等价位置。用户要求正文
   无链接时，公开文字不放链接，完整地址进证据说明；不得连溯源一起删除。

### Use form only when it shortens understanding | 只有形式能缩短理解时才使用

10. **Lists compare; tables map; figures expose relationships.** Do not add a
    form because the topic is complex. Use a short list for direct comparison, a
    compact table for definitions, conflicts, or sensitivities, and a figure when
    shape or change is harder to express in prose.
    **列表用于直比，表格用于映射，图片用于显关系。** 不因题目复杂就自动加形式。直接比较
    用短 LIST，定义／冲突／敏感性用紧凑表，形状或变化难以用散文表达时才画图。

11. **After every table or figure, give three things in plain words:** how to read
    the relevant feature; what it changes in the argument; what it cannot prove.
    Keep figures legible on the actual publication surface and inspect the rendered
    file before delivery.
    **每张表图后用白话给三件事：**怎么看关键部位；它改变了哪一步判断；它不能证明什么。
    图片须适配实际发布载体，并在交付前查看真实文件。

### Make the brief feel short because it is selective | 让短稿因选择而短

12. **Default to roughly 1,600–3,000 Chinese characters and four to six short
    sections.** Depth changes how hard the backstage work is, not how many facts
    survive into the article. User requirements override the default.
    **默认约 1600—3000 字、四至六个短节。** 深度改变后台研究难度，不决定多少事实进入
    正文。用户要求优先于默认值。

13. **Use numbered headings only for navigation.** Headings should state the
    question, mechanism, or consequence in plain language. If adjacent sections
    can swap without damage, numbering has disguised a list rather than built a
    chain.
    **编号标题只用于导航。** 小标题用白话写问题、机制或后果。相邻两节能无损调换，说明
    编号掩盖的是清单，不是因果链。

14. **Do not repeat limits paragraph by paragraph.** State each material boundary
    where it first changes the inference, then collect the decisive unknowns once
    before the conclusion. Caution should clarify, not clog.
    **不得逐段重复边界。** 每项重要边界在首次改变推断处说明，结论前再集中一次决定性未知。
    谨慎用于澄清，不用于堵塞阅读。

### Emphasize and close with restraint | 克制强调，克制收束

15. **Bold only load-bearing lines.** Reserve bold for the earned thesis,
    decisive mechanism, conditional judgment, or coda. There is no per-section
    quota, and bold never manufactures emotion.
    **只加粗承重句。** 加粗留给经证据赢得的论题、决定性机制、条件判断或收束。没有每节
    配额，也不得用加粗制造情绪。

16. **State the strongest rival and decisive unknown plainly.** Give the
    observation that distinguishes the frames. Do not let a caveat dissolve the
    judgment; do not let a judgment erase the caveat.
    **直说最强反方与决定性未知。** 写出区分竞争解释的观察。不得让边界冲淡判断，也不得让
    判断抹去边界。

17. **Close in two beats.** First give the plain conditional answer and update
    rule; then one brief contrastive coda that returns to the opening and adds no
    evidence. No emoji, clickbait, trading instruction, or flat “in summary”.
    **两拍收束。** 先给白话条件答案与更新规则，再用一句呼应开篇、且不添证据的短对照落点。
    无 emoji、标题党、交易指令或平铺的“综上”。

## One-pass clean | 一键清洗流程（成稿前）

1. Move the answer, plain reason, and decisive limit into the first screen; delete
   the method itinerary. | 把答案、白话理由与决定性边界提到第一屏；删方法路线开场。
2. Mark every sentence as observation, calculation, estimate, inference,
   scenario, or unknown; repair verbs that overstate the status. |
   给每句标观察、计算、估计、推断、情景或未知；修正夸大状态的动词。
3. Replace passive abstractions with named actors and concrete verbs; explain each
   technical term once. | 把被动抽象句换成具名主体与具象动词；术语首次出现只拆一次。
4. For each derived number, keep the intuitive mechanism and the minimum visible
   arithmetic; move nonessential derivation backstage. |
   每个推导数保留直观机制与最少可见算式；非承重推导移到后台。
5. Move sources beside facts and full addresses into the evidence note when the
   public text is link-free. | 把来源移到事实近处；正文无链接时把完整地址放入证据说明。
6. Keep only lists, tables, and figures that shorten understanding; add the
   read → meaning → limit line after each. |
   只留能缩短理解的列表、表格与图片；每个补“怎么看 → 意义 → 边界”。
7. Test adjacent sections for swapability; replace ordinal scaffolding with cause,
   consequence, or a real reader question. |
   检验相邻章节能否互换；把序号脚手架换成因果、后果或真实读者问题。
8. Delete repeated caveats, slogan chains, simulated intimacy, mood-adjectives,
   placeholders, broken links, and raw source dumps. |
   删除重复边界、金句串、模拟亲近、情绪形容词、占位符、坏链接与原始来源堆。
9. Read only the title, bold lines, and final paragraph: their certainty,
   direction, and scope must match the evidence. |
   单读标题、加粗句与末段：确定性、方向与范围必须与证据一致。
10. Synchronize every public number and claim with the current evidence and model;
    verify metadata, paths, Markdown, requested length, and non-investment note. |
    把所有公开数字与主张同步到当前证据和模型；检查元数据、路径、Markdown、目标篇幅与
    非投资建议说明。
