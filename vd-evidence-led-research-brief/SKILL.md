# Evidence-Led Research Brief · 证据驱动的精炼研究稿

## What this is | 这是什么

In one line: **a general method for doing the hard evidence work backstage and
publishing only the answer a broad reader needs — one bounded question, rival
explanations tested, calculations exposed where they matter, unknowns preserved,
and the result compressed into a brief that remains auditable.**
一句话：**一种“后台把证据做深，前台把答案讲清”的通用方法——围绕一个收窄问题检验
竞争解释，在必要处摊开计算，保留仍不可知之处，再把结果压成普通读者能读完、专业读者
能复核的精炼研究稿。**

Its register is **austere in proof, plain in delivery, short in public**. The
research may be extensive; the published brief is not a transcript of that work.
It opens on meaning, lets evidence and mechanism carry the middle, and states the
limit without turning caution into fog. It is neither academic-coded nor chatty.
它的调性是**证明冷峻、表达白话、公开成稿精炼**。研究可以很深；正文不复印研究过程。
开篇先给意思，中段由证据与机制承重，边界直说而不写成对冲迷雾。它既不学术化，也不闲聊。

Its opposite is three failures: a quick opinion with no audit trail; an evidence
dump whose answer is buried; and a false-precision brief that renames a proxy,
scenario, or residual as an observed fact.
它的反面有三种：无审计链的即兴判断；把答案埋进材料堆的研究复印件；以及把代理、情景或
残差改名成已观察事实的伪精确短稿。

## Style fingerprint | 风格指纹（九项，与 logic.md 九步一一对应）

1. **Route and bound** — name the question, audience, unit, horizon, cutoff, and
   source set; use this skill only when research must be earned before it is
   compressed for a public reader.
   **先路由、再定界**——点明问题、受众、分析单位、时间窗、截止日与来源集合；只有在
   “先研究、再压成公开短稿”时启用本 skill。
2. **Type claims, align clocks** — separate observation, calculation, estimate,
   inference, scenario, and unknown; align entity, definition, unit, denominator,
   event date, publication date, and realized-versus-forecast status.
   **主张分型、时钟对齐**——分开观察、计算、估计、推断、情景与未知；对齐主体、定义、
   单位、分母、事件日、披露日，以及实现值与预测值。
3. **Test explanations and causal edges** — let the strongest plausible account,
   at least one material rival, and a distinct null case compete; a causal arrow
   needs mechanism, timing, edge evidence, a rival, a counterfactual, lag, and a
   boundary.
   **检验解释与因果边**——让最强可信解释、至少一个实质反方及适用时的零假设同场；每条
   因果边须有机制、时序、边上证据、竞争解释、反事实、滞后与边界。
4. **Triangulate independent evidence** — trace repeated reports to their upstream
   origin, resolve conflicts by method or definition rather than vote, and keep
   source + date + location beside every load-bearing fact.
   **独立证据三角验证**——追溯重复材料的共同上游；按方法、定义或时点解决冲突，不数票；
   每个承重事实近处保留来源、日期与位置。
5. **Quantify only what is identified** — for every derived number show sourced
   inputs → arithmetic or model → result → plain meaning → largest sensitivity;
   label scenario endpoints as scenarios, never confidence bounds.
   **只量化可识别部分**——每个推导数摊开“输入出处 → 算式或模型 → 结果 → 白话含义 →
   最大敏感项”；情景端点只称情景，不冒充置信区间。
6. **Preserve the residual** — a proxy does not identify a component, an
   unexplained balance is not demand or value, and an unknown input is not zero;
   audit optimism and pessimism with the same standard.
   **保留残差**——代理不能识别具体组成，未解释余额不等于需求或价值，未知输入不等于零；
   乐观与悲观使用同一证明标准。
7. **Earn one controlling judgment** — let evidence choose the answer and its
   confidence; use a binary contrast only when it is exhaustive, otherwise name
   the remainder and the observation that would change the view.
   **赢得一个控制性判断**——让证据决定答案与置信度；只有两端穷尽时才用二分，否则点名
   剩余项，并写出会改变判断的观察。
8. **Compress for a broad reader** — give the answer, reason, and limit in the
   first screen; make each section answer one reader question; keep detailed
   method backstage and use tables or figures only when they shorten understanding.
   **压成公众短稿**——第一屏交付答案、理由与边界；每节回答一个读者问题；详细方法留在
   后台，表图只在能缩短理解时使用。
9. **Audit, synchronize, close** — verify sources, timestamps, calculations,
   repeated events, files, links, figures, Markdown, and any evidence package;
   close on a conditional judgment and one earned contrast, not a new fact.
   **审计、同步、收束**——校验来源、时间戳、计算、重复事件、文件、链接、图表、Markdown
   及按需证据包；以条件判断和一句经证据赢得的对照收尾，不添新事实。

## How to use | 怎么用

**Writing from evidence** — read [logic.md](logic.md) and complete the backstage
gates (route → type and align → compete → triangulate → quantify → preserve the
gap → judge → compress → audit). Then write per [style.md](style.md): skill
documentation in the austere voice (Voice A); the public brief in Vertex
Dimension's plain-professional voice (Voice B).
**从证据写新稿**——先读 [logic.md](logic.md)，跑完后台闸门（路由 → 分型与对齐 → 解释
竞争 → 三角验证 → 按需量化 → 保留缺口 → 判断 → 压缩 → 审计）；再依 [style.md](style.md)
落笔：skill 文档用冷峻体（Voice A），公开短稿用 Vertex Dimension 白话专业体（Voice B）。

**Reworking a draft** — audit the question and evidence before polishing the
sentences. Split facts from inferences, reconstruct the strongest rival, repair
unsupported causal edges, and move nonessential method into an evidence note.
Do not preserve a fluent sentence that the evidence cannot carry.
**重构草稿**——先审问题与证据，后润句子。拆开事实与推断，重建最强反方，修复无证因果边，
把非承重方法移到证据说明。证据承不住的流畅句，不予保留。

**When evidence does not decide** — publish the narrow answer that survives:
what is established, what remains open, and which observation would distinguish
the rivals. A brief may end in an evidence gap. It may not manufacture closure.
**证据无法裁决时**——只发布仍站得住的窄答案：什么已成立、什么仍开放、哪项观察能区分
竞争解释。短稿可以收在证据缺口；不能补造确定性。

| File | Role |
|------|------|
| [logic.md](logic.md) | Research gates, optional artifacts, stop conditions, self-check · 研究闸门、按需工件、停止条件与自检 |
| [style.md](style.md) | Skill-doc voice + public-brief voice · skill 文档体＋公开短稿体 |

The required output is one publishable Markdown brief. Evidence registers,
method notes, data, models, scripts, tables, and figures are conditional: create
only what the question and the user's requested audit depth require.
必需产物是一篇可发布的 Markdown 短稿。证据台账、方法说明、数据、模型、脚本、表格与
图片均按需生成：只创建问题与用户要求的审计深度真正需要的工件。

## One contrast | 一个最小对照

> Before (quick take) — *“Warehouse robots cut picking time by 35%, so the new
> system has raised productivity by 35%.”* The stage metric is treated as the
> system result.  
> 原写法（即兴判断）——“仓储机器人把拣货时间缩短 35%，所以整套系统的生产率提高了
> 35%。”把局部指标直接当成系统结果。

> Before (audit dump) — *“Picking −35%; packing −2%; queue +41%; order cycle
> −6%; labor hours −9% ...”* The numbers are present; the answer is not.  
> 原写法（审计堆砌）——“拣货 −35%；包装 −2%；排队 +41%；订单周期 −6%；工时 −9%……”
> 数字都在，答案不在。

> After (this skill) — *“Robots made picking much faster, but the order did not
> leave the warehouse 35% sooner. The pilot's picking stage fell 35%; end-to-end
> order time fell only 6% because the queue moved to packing. The observed gain is
> real and local. System productivity improves only if the next bottleneck also
> moves.”*  
> 改后（本 skill）——“机器人确实让拣货快了很多，但订单没有因此提前 35% 出库。试点的
> 拣货时间下降 35%，端到端订单时间只下降 6%——队列转移到了包装。局部改善已经发生；
> 只有下一个瓶颈也被移开，系统生产率才会同步提高。”

The difference is entirely: evidence first separates the local fact from the
system claim; plain language then carries only that earned distinction.
差别全在：证据先把局部事实与系统结论分开；白话只承载这条已经赢得的区别。
