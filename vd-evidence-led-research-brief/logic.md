# Evidence-Led Brief Skeleton · 证据驱动短稿推进骨架

Use this domain-neutral sequence when the public output must be short but the
answer has to be earned from heterogeneous evidence. Treat the nine steps as a
backstage protocol, not as mandatory published headings. Research can expand.
The brief must compress.
当公开产物必须精炼、而答案又须由异质证据赢得时，使用这套领域中性顺序。九步是后台协议，
不是必须照搬的文章标题。研究可以展开；短稿必须压缩。

---

## Order of advance | 推进顺序（九步研究—成稿闸门）

### 1. Route the task and bind the question | 路由任务，收窄问题

State the reader's question, audience, unit of analysis, horizon, cutoff, source
set, and desired publication length. Define the answer the reader needs — not the
largest topic the materials could support.
先定读者问题、受众、分析单位、时间窗、截止日、来源集合与目标篇幅。定义读者真正需要的
答案——不是材料能够覆盖的最大题目。

Use this skill when heterogeneous evidence must be researched or re-checked and
then compressed into a public brief. Route adjacent work away:
只有“异质证据须先研究或重核，再压成公开短稿”时使用本 skill。相邻任务须分流：

| Task shape · 任务形状 | Route · 路由 |
|---|---|
| Faithful summary or copy-edit · 忠实摘要或纯润色 | Preserve the source frame; do not add a research thesis · 保留源框架，不新增研究论题 |
| Mature synthesis → public essay · 成熟 synthesis → 公众号文稿 | Distill the supplied synthesis; do not reopen the model · 提炼既有综合研判，不重开模型 |
| One completed study led by its figures · 单一既成研究、证据逐图推进 | Explain figure by figure; do not rebuild the evidence system · 逐图解释，不重建证据系统 |
| Heterogeneous evidence → internal thesis or decision memo · 异质证据 → 内部论题或决策备忘 | Run deep inference; a public brief is not required · 完成深层推断，不强制公开短稿 |
| Heterogeneous evidence → concise public answer · 异质证据 → 精炼公开答案 | Use this skill · 使用本 skill |

Check required capabilities before the stage that needs them: current-source
retrieval for unstable facts; calculation tools for derived results; figure
generation and actual inspection only when figures are requested. A missing tool
blocks that stage; it does not authorize an invented substitute.
在对应阶段开始前检查真实能力：时效事实需要公开来源检索；推导结果需要计算能力；只有用户
要求配图时才需要制图与实际查看。能力缺失会阻断该阶段，不授权补造替代品。

### 2. Type claims and align objects, units, and clocks | 主张分型，对齐对象、单位与时钟

Build the backstage claim set before drafting:
落笔前使用固定主张状态：

| State · 状态 | Required gate · 必需闸门 |
|---|---|
| `observed` · 观察 | Exact source address and date · 精确来源位置与日期 |
| `calculated` · 计算 | Sourced inputs + visible arithmetic · 输入有出处＋算式可见 |
| `estimated` · 估计 | Model + assumptions + sensitivity · 模型＋假设＋敏感性 |
| `inferred` · 推断 | Evidence bridge + rival + falsifier · 证据桥＋竞争解释＋证伪 |
| `scenario` · 情景 | Trigger + horizon + update rule · 触发条件＋时间窗＋更新规则 |
| `unknown` · 未知 | Preserve the gap; do not fill it · 保留缺口，不补造 |

Align entity, definition, unit, currency, denominator, sample, accounting or
measurement perimeter, event date, publication date, and horizon. Separate stock
from flow, average from marginal, nominal from real, current result from guidance,
and realized input from forecast. A comparison that fails this gate stays out of
the load-bearing argument.
对齐主体、定义、单位、币种、分母、样本、会计或测量边界、事件日、披露日与时间窗。分开
存量与流量、平均与边际、名义与实际、已实现结果与指引、实现输入与预测输入。过不了此闸门
的比较不得承重。

When history is likely to anchor the answer, record a provisional judgment from
current evidence first. Read history afterward as cross-check, rival, falsifier,
or necessary extension; if it changes the judgment, name the evidence that won.
历史可能锚定方向时，先用当期证据记录临时判断，再把历史用作交叉验证、竞争解释、证伪或
必要延伸；若历史改变判断，须点名是哪组证据赢得了改变。

### 3. Let explanations compete; test every causal edge | 让解释竞争，检验每条因果边

Steelman the most plausible account: what it says, why informed people believe
it, and which observations it explains. Compare it with at least one material
rival and, when distinct, a null case. Allow the consensus, the rival, or neither
to win.
先把最可信解释强化到最强：它说什么、知情者为何相信、能解释哪些观察。再让它与至少一个
实质反方及适用时的零假设比较。允许共识胜出、反方胜出，也允许无法识别胜者。

For every `A → B`, test:
每条 `A → B` 检验：

1. temporal order · 时间顺序；
2. transmission mechanism · 传导机制；
3. evidence for the edge, not merely A and B separately · 支撑“边”而非只分别支撑 A、B 的证据；
4. rival or confounder · 替代解释或混杂；
5. counterfactual prediction · 反事实预测；
6. direction, magnitude, and lag · 方向、量级与滞后；
7. boundary or reversal condition · 边界或反转条件。

If the edge fails, call it an association or working hypothesis. Causal language
must follow identification; it cannot create it.
因果边过不了闸门，就只称相关或工作假设。因果措辞须跟在识别之后；措辞不能制造识别。

Audit positive and negative frames with the same standard. Do not require a
balanced verdict when the evidence is asymmetric; require a balanced test.
积极与消极解释使用同一证明标准。证据不对称时不强求结论正反各半；只强求检验标准对称。

### 4. Triangulate independent evidence and resolve conflicts | 三角验证独立证据，解决冲突

Build a source manifest when the corpus is nontrivial. Record source identity,
date, primary/secondary status, scope, method, and known interest. Ten retellings
of one filing count as one origin, not ten confirmations.
来源较多时建立来源清单，记录来源身份、日期、一手／二手、范围、方法与已知利益关系。同一
公告被转述十次，只算一个上游证据源，不算十次确认。

Triangulate load-bearing claims across independent origins and, where useful,
across system layers or horizons. Verify unstable or high-stakes facts against
current primary or authoritative sources. Keep source + date + page, section, or
equivalent location close to the claim; a final source list does not replace
nearby provenance.
承重主张须跨独立上游验证；条件允许时，再跨系统层级或时间窗。时效性或高风险事实对照当前
一手或权威来源。来源、日期与页码／章节／等价位置须贴近主张；文末来源清单不能替代近端溯源。

Surface conflicts and explain whether they come from date, definition, sample,
method, incentive, or genuine uncertainty. Never settle them by majority vote.
把冲突摆出来，并判断差异来自日期、定义、样本、方法、利益关系，还是真实未知。不得以多数票
消解冲突。

### 5. Quantify only what the evidence identifies | 只量化证据能够识别的部分

Do not build a model merely because the output is called research. Quantify only
when a number changes the decision and the inputs can be defended. For every
derived number show:
不要因为产物叫“研究”就强行建模。只有数字会改变判断、且输入能够辩护时才量化。每个推导数
摊开：

`sourced inputs → aligned units and periods → formula/model → result → plain meaning → largest sensitivity`
`输入出处 → 单位与期间对齐 → 算式／模型 → 结果 → 白话含义 → 最大敏感项`

Name the denominator, base period, weighting convention, and whether inputs are
realized, approximate, guided, or forecast. When a simple example helps, explain
the mechanism with a 100-unit basket before presenting the formal expression.
点明分母、基期、权重口径，以及输入属于实现值、近似值、管理层指引还是预测。需要公式时，
先用“100 单位篮子”讲清机制，再给正式表达。

Treat a range according to how it was built. A scenario grid is not a confidence
interval; a stress endpoint is not an estimate bound; a proxy index is not the
subject's transaction price. State the mapping distance and largest omitted input.
区间按其生成方式命名。情景网格不是置信区间；压力端点不是估计上下限；代理指数不是研究
对象的实际成交价。点明映射距离与最大的遗漏输入。

Never invent a probability because the format asks for one. If the evidence only
supports direction, publish direction.
不得因格式需要补造概率。证据只支持方向时，就只发布方向。

### 6. Preserve residuals, unknowns, and non-overlap | 保留残差、未知与非重叠

An attractive label is not a decomposition. A proxy applied to an unmatched
balance is a coverage scenario, not identification of that balance's contents.
An unexplained residual is not automatically demand, quality, value, fraud, or
sentiment. Unknown is a state, not zero.
一个诱人的标签不等于完成拆分。代理作用于未匹配余额，只是覆盖情景，不是识别余额组成。
未解释残差不自动等于需求、质量、价值、欺诈或情绪。未知是一种状态，不是零。

Audit double counting before drawing the insight:
推出洞见前检查重复计数：

- repeated documents with one upstream event · 同一上游事件的重复材料；
- overlapping indices or components · 重叠指数或组成；
- annual guidance reused as quarterly evidence · 年度指引重复当季度证据；
- one observation serving both model input and independent validation · 同一观察既进模型又冒充独立验证；
- one actor's outcome exported to another layer unchanged · 一个主体的结果原样外推到另一层。

When an advantage may move rather than remove a cost, name who receives the
benefit, who first pays cash, time, capacity, trust, or optionality, who can pass
it on, and which clock records the result. Map feedback only when evidence
distinguishes the loop from a plausible story.
一项优势可能只是搬走成本而非消灭成本时，点明谁受益、谁先支付现金／时间／产能／信任／
选择权、谁能转嫁，以及哪个时钟记录结果。只有证据能把反馈回路与故事区分开时才画反馈。

### 7. Earn one controlling judgment | 赢得一个控制性判断

Compress only after the prior gates. Choose the explanation that covers more
evidence with fewer unsupported assumptions. Use the fixed confidence set
`established / supported / tentative / unknown`; translate it naturally in the
published language.
跑完前述闸门后才压缩。保留覆盖更多证据、无证假设更少的解释。后台使用固定置信词面
`established / supported / tentative / unknown`，公开时自然翻译。

Write the narrowest answer the evidence earns:
把答案压到证据赢得的最窄形式：

`What is established; what better explains it; what remains unidentified; what observation would change the view.`
`什么已经成立；哪种解释力更强；什么仍无法识别；哪项观察会改变判断。`

Use one controlling question or tension through the brief. Use a binary spine
only when the alternatives are mutually exclusive and exhaustive; otherwise name
the residual instead of forcing it into either side.
全文由一个控制性问题或张力贯穿。只有两端互斥且穷尽时才使用二分脊柱；否则点名残差，
不得强塞进任一端。

The title states the earned thesis. When no frame wins, title the open question or
the established boundary. Reversal is a result, never a template.
标题陈述经证据赢得的论题。无法识别胜者时，标题写开放问题或已确定边界。反转是结果，
不是模板。

### 8. Compress the research into a public brief | 把研究压成公开短稿

Make the first screen carry the answer, its plain reason, and its decisive limit.
Do not put a source inventory or method itinerary between the reader and the
meaning. A typical Chinese brief is about 1,600–3,000 characters with four to six
short sections; user requirements and inferential depth override the default.
第一屏交付答案、白话理由与决定性边界。不要把来源清单或方法路线挡在读者与意思之间。典型
中文短稿约 1600—3000 字、四至六个短节；用户要求与推断深度优先于默认值。

Use this default public chain when it fits:
适用时采用以下公开链条：

`plain answer → why the obvious reading is incomplete → decisive evidence and bridge → strongest rival and limit → update rule → coda`
`白话答案 → 常见读法为何不完整 → 决定性证据与桥梁 → 最强反方与边界 → 更新规则 → 收束`

Each section answers the question raised by the prior section and creates the
next. Numbered headings may navigate; they may not substitute for causality. Put
detailed derivations in a method note or evidence package when the reader does not
need them to trust the conclusion.
每节回答上一节留下的问题，并逼出下一问。编号标题可以导航，不能替代因果。读者无需完整
推导即可理解结论时，把细节放进方法说明或证据包。

Use a list, table, or figure only when it makes a relationship materially easier
to grasp. After each, state how to read it, what it changes in the argument, and
what it cannot establish. Design figures for the actual publication surface;
inspect the rendered asset before delivery.
只有关系因此明显更易理解时才用列表、表格或图片。其后写清怎么看、它改变了哪一步判断、
以及不能证明什么。图片须按实际发布载体设计，并在交付前查看真实成图。

### 9. Audit, synchronize, and close | 审计、同步、收束

Run a final independent pass against the current evidence, not the fluency of the
draft. Audit historical anchoring, asymmetric standards, repeated events, proxy
overreach, stale calculations, and mismatch between evidence and title, section
endings, tone, or conclusion.
最终独立审计只对照当期证据，不被草稿流畅度说服。检查历史锚定、正负标准不对称、事件重复、
代理越权、旧计算，以及证据与标题、段尾、语气或结论的方向错配。

If the task creates multiple artifacts, validate article, source register, claim
ledger, method note, data, derived results, scripts, figures, README, and metadata
against one another. A revised model is not complete while an old claim remains in
the ledger. Verify paths, links, Markdown, timestamps, counts, units, formulas,
figure dimensions, and requested link policy.
任务生成多个工件时，交叉校验文章、来源表、主张台账、方法说明、数据、推导结果、脚本、
图片、README 与元数据。模型已更新而台账仍留旧主张，不算完成。检查路径、链接、Markdown、
时间戳、样本数、单位、公式、图片尺寸与用户要求的链接政策。

Close the analysis with the condition that would strengthen, weaken, or refute the
view. Then use one brief contrastive coda that adds no new evidence. Add a
*non-investment-advice* note when the subject is a market.
分析以会增强、削弱或推翻判断的条件收束；全文再用一句不添新证据的短对照落点。涉及市场时
标明*非投资建议*。

## Artifact contract | 工件契约

The brief is required. Everything else is conditional.
短稿必需；其余工件按需。

| Artifact · 工件 | Create when · 创建条件 | Minimum content · 最小内容 |
|---|---|---|
| Public Markdown brief · 公开 Markdown 短稿 | Always · 始终 | Earned title, answer-first opening, causal body, limit, update rule, coda · 经证据赢得的标题、答案先行开篇、因果正文、边界、更新规则、收束 |
| Source register / evidence note · 来源表／证据说明 | Sources are numerous, disputed, or link-free publication is requested · 来源多、存在争议，或正文要求无链接 | Source identity, date, location, role, independence · 来源身份、日期、位置、用途、独立性 |
| Claim ledger · 主张台账 | The brief carries several derived or contested claims · 多项推导或争议主张承重 | Claim state, evidence, confidence, status · 主张状态、证据、置信、状态 |
| Data / model / script · 数据／模型／脚本 | New calculations require reproduction · 新计算需要复算 | Sourced inputs, formula, outputs, sensitivity, validation · 输入出处、公式、输出、敏感性、校验 |
| Table / figure · 表格／图片 | A relationship is harder to grasp in prose · 关系用散文更难理解 | Readable on the target surface; takeaway and limit in body · 适配发布载体；正文给读法、落点与边界 |
| Package README · 工件包 README | Several files form one deliverable · 多文件构成一个交付包 | Scope, as-of date, file map, reproduction and limits · 范围、截止日、文件地图、复算与边界 |

Honor the user's path and metadata contract. Do not invent a project path or
artifact family merely because a prior task used one.
遵守用户指定的路径与元数据契约。不得因前一项任务使用过某种路径或工件族，就擅自沿用。

## Stop conditions | 停止条件

- The user asks for faithful summary, copy-editing, or synthesis-only projection;
  preserve that task instead of manufacturing a new thesis. |
  用户要求忠实摘要、纯润色或只投影 synthesis；保留原任务，不补造新论题。
- Sources are stale, thin, circular, or definitionally incomparable; return what
  is established and the evidence gap. |
  来源过期、过薄、循环引用或定义不可比；返回已成立部分与证据缺口。
- A causal edge lacks mechanism, temporal order, edge evidence, or a
  discriminating observation; call it association or hypothesis. |
  因果边缺机制、时序、边上证据或区分性观察；只称相关或假设。
- A derived number lacks a denominator, aligned period, source input, visible
  arithmetic, or sensitivity; do not publish it as a result. |
  推导数缺分母、对齐期间、输入出处、可见算式或敏感性；不得作为结果发布。
- A proxy does not match the relevant product, population, transaction, or time;
  label the mapping gap or do not use it. |
  代理与相关产品、总体、交易或时点不匹配；点明映射缺口，或不用。
- The result depends on calling a residual by the desired answer; stop at partial
  identification. |
  结果依赖把残差改名成想要的答案；止于部分识别。
- A required current-source, calculation, or rendering capability is unavailable;
  report the blocked stage rather than substitute silently. |
  必需的当前来源、计算或渲染能力不可用；报告被阻断阶段，不得静默替代。

## Self-check | 自检清单

- [ ] Is this genuinely a deep-evidence-to-public-brief task, not a summary,
      synthesis projection, pure rewrite, or single-study explanation? |
      这是否确为“深证据 → 公开短稿”，而非摘要、综合研判投影、纯改写或单一研究解释？
- [ ] Are the question, audience, unit, horizon, cutoff, and source set explicit? |
      问题、受众、分析单位、时间窗、截止日与来源集合是否明确？
- [ ] Are observation, calculation, estimate, inference, scenario, and unknown
      separated? | 观察、计算、估计、推断、情景与未知是否分开？
- [ ] Are entity, definition, unit, denominator, sample, dates, and
      realized-versus-forecast status aligned? |
      主体、定义、单位、分母、样本、日期及实现／预测状态是否对齐？
- [ ] Could the strongest rival genuinely have won, and does each causal edge pass
      mechanism, time, edge evidence, counterfactual, lag, and boundary? |
      最强反方是否真有可能胜出；每条因果边是否通过机制、时序、边上证据、反事实、滞后与边界？
- [ ] Are repeated reports traced to independent origins and conflicts resolved by
      definition or method rather than vote? |
      重复材料是否追溯到独立上游；冲突是否按定义或方法而非多数票解决？
- [ ] Does every derived number show sourced inputs, aligned arithmetic, meaning,
      and largest sensitivity? |
      每个推导数是否给出输入出处、对齐算式、含义与最大敏感项？
- [ ] Are scenario endpoints, proxy mappings, residuals, and unknown inputs named
      honestly, with no double counting? |
      情景端点、代理映射、残差与未知输入是否如实命名，且无重复计数？
- [ ] Does one controlling judgment follow from the evidence, with confidence,
      strongest limit, falsifier, and update rule? |
      是否由证据推出一个控制性判断，并点明置信、最大边界、证伪与更新规则？
- [ ] Does the first screen give the answer, plain reason, and decisive limit? |
      第一屏是否交付答案、白话理由与决定性边界？
- [ ] Does each section answer the prior question; do lists, tables, and figures
      change understanding and carry a takeaway plus limit? |
      每节是否回答上节问题；列表、表格与图片是否改变理解，并带落点与边界？
- [ ] Are only load-bearing sentences bolded; are terms explained once and sources
      kept near claims without a number wall? |
      是否只加粗承重句；术语是否只解释一次；来源是否贴近主张且无数字墙？
- [ ] If multiple artifacts exist, do article, evidence, calculations, figures,
      README, metadata, and paths agree exactly? |
      多工件存在时，文章、证据、计算、图片、README、元数据与路径是否完全一致？
- [ ] Does the brief close on an update condition and an earned coda, with no new
      evidence, hype, or investment advice? |
      短稿是否以更新条件与经证据赢得的收束结束，且不添新证据、不炒作、不构成投资建议？
